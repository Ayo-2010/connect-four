How to win

Connect four discs in a row — sideways, upright or diagonally. The centre columns are prime real estate.

How to play

Take turns dropping discs into the grid. The first player to line up four in a row wins the round.

  1. Choose Vs Computer or 2 Players.
  2. Click a column, or press 1 to 7, to drop your disc.
  3. A disc falls to the lowest free slot in the column you pick.
  4. Line up four of your colour across, down or diagonally to win.
  5. The computer takes the centre when it can and blocks your lines when it must.
  6. Press N for a new game. The session score and best streak are kept.
